Time in Status 3.0.0 for Jira Cloud shows where work waits, how delivery changes over time and which open issues need attention. Reports use your Jira workflow, Jira history and an optional working calendar.

## Choose your question

| Your question | Report to use |
| --- | --- |
| Where does work wait? | **Time in status** |
| Where did workflow time occur each week or month? | **Status time by period** |
| How long does delivery take? | **Cycle time**, **Lead time** or **Delivery trend** |
| Which current issues have been waiting longest? | **Open work aging** or **Current visit age** |
| Who owned work, and in which status? | **Assignee time** or **Assignee by status** |
| How often did work enter or move between statuses? | **Status count** or **Transition count** |
| How long did Priority, Sprint or another supported field hold a value? | **Jira field duration** or **Jira field value count** |

You can compare results by one or two dimensions, use total, mean or median aggregation, and drill into contributing issues. Status summaries also show distribution statistics such as P85 where available.

Saved reports keep configurations rather than frozen results. Sharing a configuration or showing it in the dashboard gadget never grants additional Jira issue access; each viewer calculates results with their own permissions.

**Assignee metrics measure elapsed ownership, not hours worked or individual productivity.** Timesheets and billing are outside the app's scope.
