---
title: "Support"
description: "Get help with LaTeX rendering, references, exports, or Forge upgrades."
---

Submit bugs and feature requests through the [Fulstech Support portal](https://fulstech.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portals).

To help us investigate efficiently, include:

- Your Confluence Cloud site URL.
- The macro name and whether it uses MathJax or TeX Live.
- The LaTeX source, with confidential values removed if necessary.
- What you expected and what happened instead.
- Whether the issue affects the page, PDF export, Word export, or all three.
- For references, whether the target is on the same page, another page, or a child page.
- A screenshot and one affected page URL when possible.

For an upgrade issue, also include the installed major version shown under **Manage apps**. Existing major 5 installations require site-admin approval before upgrading to Forge major 6.
