**Better Pages Home** is the starting point for templates and page-building
tools. Open **Apps → Better Pages** to browse 49 starters across project
management, reports, research, product, homepages, documentation, people,
meetings, decisions, strategy, agile, and operations.

## Create from a template

1. Search by purpose or choose a category.
2. Select a template to review its description and section outline.
3. Choose the destination space and enter a page or blog title.
4. Check the Confluence permission result.
5. Select **Create draft**.
6. Complete the private draft in the normal Confluence editor, then publish it.

The template provides a useful structure and prompts, not locked content. Delete,
reorder, or rewrite any section to fit the real audience.

## Create a delivery journey

**Delivery journey** creates a published landing page and up to 12 linked child
pages from a reviewed list of stage names. Because it writes several pages,
review the destination, titles, and hierarchy before confirming.

## Make templates available in Confluence

A Confluence administrator can open Better Pages administration and synchronize
selected page starters into the native Confluence template catalog. Authors can
then find those starters in Confluence's normal **Create from template** flow.
Blog and multi-page journey starters remain available from Better Pages Home.

## Good practice

- Choose the smallest starter that matches the outcome.
- Replace every prompt with real content before publishing.
- Check owners, restrictions, labels, and review dates on the created page.
- Treat Delivery journey as a real write operation, not a preview.
