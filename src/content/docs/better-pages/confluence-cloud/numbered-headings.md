---
title: "Numbered Headings"
description: "Apply and maintain structured numbering on a Confluence page's headings."
---

**Numbered Headings** adds visible prefixes to supported headings on one page or
blog. Open it from the Better Pages byline action on content you can edit.

## Apply numbering

1. Open the published page and select **Numbered Headings** in the byline.
2. Choose decimal, Latin alphabet, or Roman numeral numbering.
3. Decide how skipped heading levels should be handled.
4. Preview the heading outline.
5. Apply the change and reload the page to verify the result.

Better Pages writes the prefixes into heading text so they remain readable in
normal Confluence views. Page-update handling reapplies the chosen scheme after
later edits; synchronization can take up to three minutes.

Choose **Remove numbering** to remove prefixes managed by Better Pages. Review
the preview carefully if authors have manually typed similar prefixes.

## Good practice

- Use heading levels for structure before adding numbers.
- Keep one numbering scheme for the complete page.
- Recheck links or references that quote full heading text.
- If the action says the feature is disabled, ask a site administrator to enable
  Numbered Headings in Better Pages administration.
