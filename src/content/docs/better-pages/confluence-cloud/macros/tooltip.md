---

Use **Better Pages Tooltip** for a short definition or clarification that would
interrupt the main sentence. New tooltips render as a compact information icon.

## Add a Tooltip

1. Insert **Better Pages Tooltip** next to the term or control it explains.
2. Enter concise tooltip text.
3. Save the macro and publish the page.

## Reader interaction

Pointer users see the explanation after a short hover. Keyboard users receive
the same behavior on focus. Clicking or tapping the icon opens the explanation
in a Confluence-hosted dialog, making it usable on touch devices.

The tooltip does not target or modify another page element. Place it directly
beside the content it explains.

## Good practice

- Keep the explanation to one or two short sentences.
- Do not put mandatory instructions only in a tooltip.
- Use the surrounding text to identify the subject; avoid repeating it.
- Verify hover, focus, click, Escape, and focus return on the published page.
