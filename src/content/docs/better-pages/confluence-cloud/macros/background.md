---

Use **Better Pages Background** to give a section a clear visual boundary without
turning its content into an image. The macro body remains normal Confluence
content and stays readable, editable, and searchable.

[![Published Product Launch Hub section with a light surface, rounded corners, heading, introductory text, and three cards](../images/release-13/getting-started-reader.jpg)](/assets/better-pages/release-13/getting-started-reader.jpg)

Release 13 example: the Background provides a shared surface for editable rich
content and the Plan, Launch, and Support navigation cards.

## Add a Background

1. Insert **Better Pages Background** and enter an accessible section label.
2. Choose a surface color and let Better Pages select readable text, or set the
   text color manually.
3. Set side spacing, minimum height, corner radius, and content alignment.
4. Optionally add built-in artwork, a Brand Kit asset, an upload, a page
   attachment, or a licensed stock image.
5. Save the macro, add rich content to its body, and publish the page.

## Image and layout controls

Images can use natural size, contain, or cover. Choose a focal position and
image opacity; add a surface color behind the image to preserve contrast. The
section can wrap its content or use a medium, large, or custom minimum height.

## Example

Create a product hero with a cover image, centered content, generous side
spacing, a short heading, one sentence, and a [Button](../button/). Keep the
image decorative unless it communicates information not present in the text.

## Good practice

- Use an accessible label that describes the section's purpose.
- Check text contrast over both the brightest and darkest parts of an image.
- Avoid fixed tall sections for short content, especially on phones.
- Publish and review the real page at desktop and narrow widths.
