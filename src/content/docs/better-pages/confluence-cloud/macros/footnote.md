Use **Better Pages Footnote** for a source note or short explanation that would
distract from the main sentence.

## Add a footnote

1. Insert **Better Pages Footnote** immediately after the relevant text.
2. Enter the note, up to 2,000 characters.
3. Add a [Footnote Summary](../footnote-summary/) later on the page.
4. Publish and check the assigned number and summary link.

Numbers are assigned from the order of published Footnotes. A marker shows a
preview on hover or keyboard focus and links to the collected note.

The next Footnote Summary collects Footnotes above it, stopping at the previous
summary in the same page scope.

## Good practice

- Keep the note focused; use a child page for substantial detail.
- Place the macro directly after the statement it supports.
- Always include a summary for a complete keyboard and reading path.
- Recheck numbering after moving or deleting references.
