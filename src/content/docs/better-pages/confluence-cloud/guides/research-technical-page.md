---
title: "Build a research or technical page"
description: "Present evidence, conclusions, and actionable technical guidance clearly."
---

**Outcome:** A reader can tell what was investigated, what the evidence says,
and what to do or verify next.

The shipped **Research brief** template includes question, method and data,
findings, interpretation, and references. For a procedure, use the
**Documentation page** template instead. **Metrics and evidence** and
**Technical reference** are shipped [Section Patterns](../../section-patterns/);
the latter uses native headings and optional code supplied by the author.

1. From **Better Pages home**, preview the template that matches the reader's
   task and create a private draft.
2. Replace prompts with the question, method or prerequisites, dated evidence,
   conclusion, and stable references. State uncertainty in your own findings.
3. In [Composer](../../smart-designer/), add **Metrics and evidence** for a
   small comparable set of measures, or **Technical reference** for ordered
   guidance. Preview the generated content before adding it to the canvas.
4. Save and reopen. Check that tables, code, and references remain readable,
   then publish for the intended audience.

**Finished-page check:** Give tables clear headings and explain measurements
in text. Test links, any [LaTeX](../../macros/latex-builder/) content, and
wide tables at a narrow browser width and with keyboard navigation.
