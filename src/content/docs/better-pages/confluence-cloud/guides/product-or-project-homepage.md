---
title: "Build a product or project homepage"
description: "Give stakeholders one maintained entry point for status, plans, and support."
---

**Outcome:** A reader can identify the purpose, current state, owner, and next
useful destination of a product or project.

[![Published Product Launch Hub introduction offers Plan, Launch, and Support destinations](../images/release-13/getting-started-reader.jpg)](/assets/better-pages/release-13/getting-started-reader.jpg)

*A finished **Find your way** section, built from a blank private draft and
explicitly published on the development site. This illustrates the navigation
part of a homepage, not the entire Product or service homepage template.
[Getting Started](../../getting-started/) follows this same example.*

Choose the shipped **Product or service homepage** template for a product, or
**Project brief** for a bounded project. The **Project status** and **Find your
way** [Section Patterns](../../section-patterns/) can add a current update
and a small navigation area. Use [Advanced Cards](../../macros/advanced-cards/)
only when descriptions help readers choose among destinations.

1. In **Apps → Better Pages home → Browse templates**, preview the relevant
   template and create a private draft in the right space.
2. Replace prompts with the audience, scope, owner, current status, and links
   to the plan, roadmap, decision log, and support. Give the status a date or
   next-update expectation in your own content.
3. Select **Continue designing**. Add **Project status** if readers need an
   immediate state and action; add **Find your way** when there are several
   destinations. Preview and review in [Composer](../../smart-designer/).
4. Save the draft, reopen it, verify the destinations and ownership, then
   publish.

**Finished-page check:** The status should be readable as text without relying
on a colored indicator. Keep navigation labels specific, test every action by
keyboard, and inspect the page at a narrow browser width.
