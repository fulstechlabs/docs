---
title: "Privacy Policy"
---

Fulstech apps keep customer data in Atlassian products whenever the app's
functionality can run entirely on Atlassian infrastructure. We do not operate a
customer-content database and we do not sell customer data.

Some apps use a Fulstech-operated service for a specific processing task, such
as rendering user-provided markup into an image. When an app uses such a
service, its Atlassian Marketplace privacy details disclose the processing and
the data involved. These services:

* receive only the content and options required to perform the requested task;
* use encrypted HTTPS connections;
* do not receive Atlassian passwords, API tokens, or customer-supplied service
  credentials;
* do not persist customer content in a database or object store; and
* remove per-request temporary files after processing. Derived output may be
  retained in a bounded, volatile service cache until eviction or service
  termination.

Fulstech-operated processing services are part of the app and are not
customer-selected third-party integrations. We do not transfer customer content
to unrelated third-party systems. Atlassian continues to govern data stored in
Atlassian products under Atlassian's own privacy terms.

For questions about a particular app or data flow, contact us through the
[Fulstech support portal](https://fulstech.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portals).
