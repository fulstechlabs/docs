---
title: "Support"
---

Please go to <https://fulstech.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portals> to submit bugs and feature requests.

For the Forge successor, first check the project/issue-type mapping in
**Project settings → JEditor Fields**, your Jira browse/edit permissions,
and whether you are using the [Forge guide](../forge-successor/) rather than
the legacy Connect instructions.

For **Saved; sync pending** or **Saved; sync required**, preserve the issue
content and avoid uninstalling the app or clearing values as a repair step.
If the message persists after a later load, contact support.

Include your app version/platform, relevant field type, reproduction steps,
expected/actual result, and sanitized error text or screenshot. Do not send
passwords, API tokens, unnecessary customer content, or raw network/log files
containing credentials. See [Security and data](../security-and-data/).
